Genosis

The Battle of Genosis was the first major battle of the Clone Wars. It was fought between the Grand Army of the Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems on the planet Genosis.

Early War

The early stages of the Clone Wars were marked by intense fighting and strategic maneuvers. Specifically on Christophsis, the Republic faced significant challenges as they attempted to gain control of the planet.

Fog of War

The Battle of Umbara was a significant battle in the Clone Wars that took place on the planet Umbara. It was known for its intense fighting and the use of advanced technology by both sides. It reflects the fog of war highlighting the republics leadership struggles and the challenges of fighting in unfamiliar terrain.

Galaxy Loses Faith in Jedi

After the bombing of the Jedi Temple, the galaxy begins to lose faith in the Jedi Order. This event marks a turning point in the Clone Wars, as it leads to increased tension and mistrust between the Jedi and the Republic.

Order 66

The Clone Wars came to a dramatic end with the execution of Order 66, which instructed the clone troopers to eliminate the Jedi and their allies. In the image above we see emperor Palpatine giving the order to clone commander Cody. This event marked the fall of the order as thousands of Jedi were killed and the Republic was transformed into the Galactic Empire. It also set the stage for the return of the Sith.
